Quick questions

Do you deliver?

Yes. Build your basket, choose “Delivery” and add your neighborhood or ZIP: the store confirms the area, fee and time on WhatsApp.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. Send your list on WhatsApp with the time you’ll stop by and the store packs it.

Will you cut the meat my way?

At the butcher counter you can choose whole, steaks or thin sliced.

Open on holidays?

Special hours go out on Instagram and the deals group. On July 4th, for example, the store opened 8am–4pm.
